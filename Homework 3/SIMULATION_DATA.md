# Simulation dataset provenance

The user supplied Himass and TanVuu as case-study inspiration. These strings are **reference labels only**, not the subjects of factual findings in this application. No external case material was supplied/verified and no real-world accusation, evidence item or sanction is asserted.

`sim-himass` and `sim-tanvuu` identify invented Player Alpha/SIM-HM and Player Beta/SIM-TV records. Teams, roles, summaries, dates, replay/document/statement references and outcomes are fictional training content. `simulation://` references are identifiers displayed as text, not links to supposed source evidence. Avatars use local initials; no real person's photograph is used.

Every player/dossier/evidence/sanction has simulation=true, each outcome status is DEMONSTRATION, and the snapshot includes the shared explicit simulation notice. Fixtures are deeply frozen. `validateDataset` checks identifiers, relationship ownership, labels, UTC chronology, evidence kinds/provenance and sanction consistency; it does not decide real culpability or legal eligibility.

Fixture dates are fixed for a repeatable exercise. Displayed deadlines are illustrative absolute UTC dates, not an active authoritative submission window. No appeal form, SLA countdown or persistence is implemented in this scope.
